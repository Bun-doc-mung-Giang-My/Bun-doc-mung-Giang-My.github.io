A note from Dana

How we work with families

I started this because I wanted tutoring to feel personal — not like a big test-prep chain. When you send a booking, it comes straight to me. I take a moment to match your family with the right tutor, confirm the time with you, and stay reachable if anything changes.

We keep things small on purpose. Six tutors, real relationships, and a focus on helping your child feel confident again.

— Dana
